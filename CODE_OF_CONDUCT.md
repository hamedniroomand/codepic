# Code of conduct

CodePic is a small project, and everyone taking part in it is expected to keep it friendly.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you disagree.
- Keep feedback about the code and the idea, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes into the project.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Trolling, or anything else that would be out of place in a workplace

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer through the links on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/hamedniroomand). Reports are kept private.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block people who keep breaking them.
